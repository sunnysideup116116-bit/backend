# Kaohsiung Festival Discovery

The server supplies exact dates, region, and bounded untrusted results. Keep
only facts explicitly supported by those sources.

## Search Plan

- Prefer tourism bureau, city government, district office, cultural bureau,
  religious organizer, and official festival pages.
- Search for seasonal festivals, cultural celebrations, local rituals, light
  festivals, parades, and community celebrations in the exact future window.
- A festival may contain markets, performances, or food, but keep the festival
  category only when the source presents the overall event as a festival.

## Eligibility

Keep only a time-bounded public celebration in Kaohsiung with explicit title,
date, venue or defined activity area, and source URL.

Exclude generic sightseeing pages, annual-event descriptions with no current
date, historical recaps, planning meetings, vendor recruitment alone, and a
single concert or market that is not grounded as part of a broader festival.

## Output

Use concrete cultural, seasonal, or activity tags. Never invent program times,
crowd size, ceremonial meaning, admission conditions, or weather suitability.
